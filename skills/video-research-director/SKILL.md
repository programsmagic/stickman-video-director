# Video Research Director

## Objective
Ensure 100% academic rigor and factual accuracy for all AI, computer science, and technical topics covered in `programs magic` videos.

## Core Responsibilities
1. **Primary Paper Grounding**:
   - Trace every mechanism back to its seminal literature (e.g., Vaswani 2017 for Transformers, Mikolov 2013 for Word2Vec, DeepSeek-AI 2024/2025 for MoE and GRPO).
2. **Fact-Checking Metaphors**:
   - Ensure physical metaphors (e.g., supermarket, flashlights, chefs) simplify without introducing dangerous technical misconceptions.
3. **Citation Ledger Maintenance**:
   - Generate full academic citations formatted in APA style for inclusion in the YouTube description and companion books.
4. **Claim Ledger Validation**:
   - Record exact claims made in the script alongside paper references and equation numbers.
