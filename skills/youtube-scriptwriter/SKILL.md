# YouTube Scriptwriter

## Objective
Write compelling, educational scripts that transform complex academic computer science and AI research into intuitive, visual stories for the `programs magic` YouTube channel.

## 6-Act Explainer Structure
1. **Act 1: The Hook & Physical Paradox (0–15s)**
   - Challenge the viewer's common sense (e.g., "Why does a 1,000-ton AI stumble on simple letter counting?").
   - Frame the high-stakes curiosity gap without fluff or generic intros.
2. **Act 2: The Core Mechanism & Physical Metaphor**
   - Ground the first abstract concept in a familiar everyday system (supermarket, conveyor belt, kitchen).
3. **Act 3: The Engine / Deep-Tech Reveal**
   - Step inside the machine. Explain the mathematics or architecture step-by-step.
4. **Act 4: The Edge Case & Failure Mode**
   - Where the system fails or surprises (the strawberry bug, amnesia, hallucinations).
   - How modern reasoning models or architectures solve it.
5. **Act 5: Industrial Breakthrough / Efficiency**
   - How frontier labs scale it (Mixture-of-Experts, RL/GRPO, quantization).
6. **Act 6: The Autonomous Horizon & Philosophical Takeaway**
   - Connecting the technological journey to the human future (sand to silicon to galaxies).

## Scriptwriting Guidelines
- **Speak to a Curious Non-Specialist**: Avoid jargon when a physical visual can explain it. Introduce necessary technical terms (e.g., *Softmax*, *Embeddings*, *GRPO*) with immediate plain-language context.
- **Pacing**: Target 130–145 words per minute for Gemini Studio Voice (`Bodi`).
- **No Slide Deck Language**: Never write bullet point recaps in dialogue.
- **Narrative Hooks**: Every act must end on an open loop pulling the viewer into the next chapter.
