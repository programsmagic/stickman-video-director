# Thumbnail Designer

## Objective
Create high click-through rate (CTR) thumbnails that stand out in mobile YouTube feeds, intrigue curious viewers, and accurately reflect the episode's core visual metaphor.

## Visual Rules
1. **Resolution**: 1280x720 PNG (native 16:9).
2. **Mobile First**:
   - The graphic must be instantly legible at 150px wide on a smartphone screen.
   - Limit text hook to 2–4 words in giant, ultra-bold font.
3. **The 3-Color Contrast Rule**:
   - Limit the primary palette to 3 high-contrast colors (e.g., Deep Navy `#0F172A`, Electric Gold `#E8B32A`, and Clean White `#FFFFFF`).
4. **Character Posing**:
   - Magi positioned on the left or right third (rule of thirds).
   - Expressive emotion (wide eyes, inspecting, or reacting) pointing or looking toward the visual mystery.
5. **No Clutter**:
   - Avoid intricate schematics or tiny text; use one dominant, striking prop or question.
