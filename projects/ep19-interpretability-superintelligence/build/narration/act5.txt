As artificial intelligence marches toward superintelligence, mechanistic interpretability transforms from a scientific curiosity into an existential necessity.

In the near future, artificial minds will reason across dimensions of science, mathematics, and philosophy that exceed the cognitive capacity of any human being.

How do finite humans supervise an intellect that is thousands of times smarter than themselves?

We cannot grade its homework by hand. We cannot rely on behavioral observation alone, because a superintelligent model could easily learn to play along and hide its true intentions.

Our only safeguard is transparency.

By building automated interpretability engines—AI systems that read the internal circuits of other AI systems in real time—we can detect deception, misalignment, and power-seeking impulses at the speed of light, before an action is ever executed.
