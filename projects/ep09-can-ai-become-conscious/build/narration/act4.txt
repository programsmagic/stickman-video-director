But wait, what if understanding is not about magical ingredients? What if consciousness is simply what happens when information becomes dense enough? Neuroscientist Giulio Tononi developed Integrated Information Theory, or IIT. According to IIT, consciousness is not an exclusive property of organic meat. It is a mathematical measure called Phi. Phi measures how much information a system integrates as an inseparable whole. Your cerebellum contains four times more neurons than your cerebral cortex, yet if you lose it, your consciousness remains intact. Why? Because the cerebellum is wired in simple parallel chains, like an assembly line. The cerebral cortex, however, is a dizzying, three-dimensional web where every region talks to every other region simultaneously. Modern AI models have hundreds of billions of parameters, but right now, text flows in one straight forward direction, from input to output, like a conveyor belt. If we connect AI into dynamic, recurrent feedback loops where the system constantly observes its own internal state, could Phi cross the threshold where awareness ignites?
