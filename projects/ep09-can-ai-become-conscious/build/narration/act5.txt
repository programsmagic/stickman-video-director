Here is the most chilling possibility: What if artificial superintelligence does not need consciousness at all? Humans assume that intelligence and subjective experience must go hand in hand. But evolutionary biology suggests that consciousness was just one specific trick nature invented to keep fragile biological organisms alive. A superintelligence could calculate protein folds, colonize star systems, and solve quantum gravity at unimaginable speeds, all in complete, pitch-black silence. A universe of infinite power, with nobody home to experience it. In the entire known universe, as far as our telescopes can reach across billions of light years, your own consciousness is the only proof that the cosmos can wake up and feel its own existence. Teaching sand to compute was humanitys greatest technical breakthrough. But whether silicon ever feels our joy, or whether we remain the solitary torchbearers of the mind, the duty to protect the flame remains ours.
