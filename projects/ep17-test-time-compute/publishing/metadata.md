## YouTube Description

Why did AI suddenly get shockingly good at math, logic, and coding in 2024 and 2025?

For years, large language models suffered from a fatal defect: they were physically incapable of pausing to think. In human psychology, Daniel Kahneman divided thought into System 1 (fast, instinctive intuition) and System 2 (slow, deliberate deduction). Standard LLMs—from GPT-3 to GPT-4—operated purely as System 1. Every single word cost the exact same split-second forward pass, whether saying hello or proving an impossible math theorem.

Then came the discovery of the Second Scaling Law: Test-Time Compute. 

Instead of demanding an instant answer, frontier reasoning models (OpenAI o1, DeepSeek-R1) generate thousands of internal reasoning tokens before showing a single word to the human. In this masterclass episode, Stickman Magi takes you inside the thinking machine: how hidden scratchpads work, why Process Reward Models (PRMs) outperform outcome graders, how DeepSeek-R1 discovered self-reflection without human demonstrations, and how Monte Carlo Tree Search is turning AI from autocomplete into engines of scientific discovery.
