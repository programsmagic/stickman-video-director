But how does the model know what to sculpt? Why an astronaut and not a bowl of fruit? This is where text conditioning enters the equation. Words from your prompt are transformed into numeric vectors and injected directly into the U-Net through cross-attention layers. The words act like an invisible magnetic field, bending the trajectory of denoising. But left on its own, the model often produces dreamy, washed-out images that casually ignore half your prompt. To fix this, researchers invented Classifier-Free Guidance. At every step, the network makes two separate predictions: one listening to your text prompt, and one completely ignoring it. It then subtracts the unconditional drift and dramatically amplifies the difference. Crank the guidance dial to seven point five, and the machine charges aggressively toward your exact words.
