When you ask an AI model a question, it does not read like a human. It does not read one word at a time.
In 2017, eight researchers at Google published a paper titled "Attention Is All You Need."
They introduced the Transformer.
Instead of reading sequentially, a transformer looks at every word in a sentence simultaneously.
It calculates mathematical relationships, or attention weights, between every single token at the speed of light.
Scale that equation up with supercomputers, and you get modern language models and autonomous agents.
