In 1969, two MIT scientists killed artificial intelligence research for twenty years using a single math problem.
Early neural networks could solve simple logic. But when researchers tested an XOR gate, the system crashed.
Why? Because an XOR problem is mathematically non-linear. You cannot separate its points with a single straight line.
In 1973, governments concluded AI had failed its promises. DARPA slashed funding, plunging the world into the brutal First AI Winter.
It took twenty years for researchers to realize: neural networks weren't broken. We just needed more layers.
