For sixty years, AI was an analyzer: it classified images, filtered spam, and predicted numbers.
Then came the generative explosion. In 2020, diffusion models flipped the paradigm. By training neural networks to remove noise from blurred images, computers learned to reverse the process: hallucinating high-definition artwork, photorealistic faces, and music from pure noise.
By 2024, diffusion models merged with transformers to create video generation engines like Sora and Hunyuan. Computers were no longer just reading descriptions. They were simulating three-dimensional physical motion, reflections, and light.
