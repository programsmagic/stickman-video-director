For seventy years, AI only analyzed what humans gave it. It tagged photos and predicted numbers.
Then in 2020, scientists taught computers how to dream using diffusion.
Imagine taking a crystal-clear photograph and slowly sprinkling static noise over it until it looks like television snow.
Scientists taught a neural network how to reverse that snow step by step—wiping away the noise until a brand new, photorealistic image appears out of thin air.
By 2024, diffusion merged with transformers to create video generators like Sora.
Computers weren't just reading text anymore. They were imagining 3D worlds, simulating reflections, water ripples, and gravity.
