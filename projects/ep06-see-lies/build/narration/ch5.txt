Midnight arrives on the seventh day.
The glowing tags fade into the quiet night air.
Over the last six weeks, you have experienced every impossible power:
You saw the salaries of every neighbor and realized wealth buys comfort, not peace.
You saw the karma scores and learned that morality is an inward discipline, not a public spectacle.
You heard what people whispered behind your back and found the freedom of not needing applause.
You tasted infinite save points and discovered that life's beauty exists only because mistakes are permanent.
You saw the countdown clocks of mortality and learned that our limited time is what makes every moment sacred.
And now, seeing every lie has taught you the ultimate lesson:
Truth without empathy is cruelty.
And honesty without vulnerability is just another weapon.
You step outside onto the morning sidewalk.
The barista hands you your cup and says, have a wonderful day.
There is no green tag. There is no red tag.
Just two strangers on a spinning rock, trying to be kind to each other.
You smile back at her, take a sip of hot coffee, and whisper:
Thank you.
And this time, in your heart, you know it is the absolute truth.
