Because tokenizers are trained on raw internet scrapes before the neural network is ever taught to think, they create terrifying ghosts in the machine. In 2023, AI researchers uncovered a bizarre list of words that caused GPT models to malfunction, speak in tongues, or spit hostile insults. The most famous was a token named: "SolidGoldMagikarp". It was the username of an avid Pokémon fan on Reddit. Because the username appeared thousands of times on Reddit forums, the BPE tokenizer gave it a permanent slot in its vocabulary dictionary. But during the actual AI training phase, Reddit forums were filtered out! The neural network had a dial for "SolidGoldMagikarp", but its weight was never tuned. It became an unanchored negative vector in high-dimensional space—a semantic landmine waiting to be stepped on.
