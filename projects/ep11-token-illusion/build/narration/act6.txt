AI pioneers know the tokenizer is a temporary crutch. Andre Karpathy famously described tokenization as the single most frustrating source of bugs in deep learning. Now, frontier labs are building Token-Free models—architectures like Byte Latent Transformers that ingest pure binary byte streams directly, grouping entropy dynamically like a human eye reading fluid handwriting. When AI breaks free of the token illusion, models will count letters effortlessly, translate low-resource languages at equal speed, and perceive text without distortion. Until then, remember: When you speak to an AI, it doesn't hear your words. It is merely assembling the wooden jigsaw of humanity's fragmented past.
