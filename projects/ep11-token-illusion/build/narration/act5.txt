The token illusion doesn't just create spelling bugs. It creates a global economic divide. Tokenizers are overwhelmingly trained on English internet text. When you write in English, an entire sentence might condense into just four tokens. But when someone writes in Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, or Swahili, the tokenizer doesn't recognize the subwords. It is forced to shatter the text into raw Unicode bytes. A sentence that costs four cents to process in English can cost twenty-eight cents in Hindi—and run seven times slower. This invisible inefficiency is known as the Token Tax. A hidden tariff on the non-English-speaking world hardcoded into the arithmetic of modern AI.
