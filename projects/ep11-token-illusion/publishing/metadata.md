## YouTube Description

When you type into ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude, you assume the machine reads the letters just like you do.

In reality, modern AI is completely, utterly blind to letters.

Before a neural network ever sees your prompt, your text is fed into a linguistic guillotine called a Tokenizer. It chops your words into chunky, irregular puzzle blocks called tokens.

Here is the complete visual breakdown of the Token Illusion—why AI fails the "strawberry" spelling test, how Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) compresses human language, the bizarre story of the "SolidGoldMagikarp" glitch token, the global 700% Token Tax on non-English languages, and how new Token-Free models will finally teach machines to perceive continuous thought.
