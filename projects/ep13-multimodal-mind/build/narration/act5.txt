Until recently, even the best voice assistants were a Frankenstein pipeline. First, a speech model transcribed your voice into text. Then a language model read the text and typed a response. Finally, a text-to-speech model read the reply out loud. Every step added latency. Worse, you lost all the music of human conversation. Sarcasm, whispers, emotional tension, interruptions—all crushed under the wheels of the pipeline. That all changed with native omnimodality in models like GPT-4o and Gemini. There is no separate transcriber. There is no text translator. A single neural network ingests video frames, sound waves, and text tokens simultaneously into a shared attention stream. It hears the pitch in your voice, sees the expression on your face, and responds in under three hundred milliseconds with natural, expressive human prosody.
