Even with backpropagation, standard gradient descent gets stuck in flat valleys where the slope is almost zero. To solve this, physicists gave the algorithm Momentum. Instead of treating the AI like a weightless speck of dust that stops the instant the slope levels out, we treat it like a heavy iron boulder rolling down the slope. As it rolls, it builds velocity. When it hits a flat plateau or a tiny bump, its momentum carries it straight through until gravity grabs it again. Frontier AI labs combine this with adaptive optimizers like Adam. Think of it as giving the hiker smart hydraulic boots that automatically take huge strides across flat plains, but shrink down to micro-precision steps when balancing on razor-sharp ledges.
