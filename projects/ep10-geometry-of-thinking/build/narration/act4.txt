How does the mountain calculate which direction is downhill for billions of dials at the same time? It uses Backpropagation. Invented in its modern form in the nineteen-eighties, backprop is nothing more than the mathematical chain rule of calculus transformed into an industrial pulley system. When the AI makes a prediction at the output layer, we calculate how far off it was. Then, that error signal travels backward through the neural network like a shockwave. Each layer tells the layer behind it: "Here is exactly how much you contributed to my mistake. Turn your dial three degrees left." In a fraction of a millisecond, billions of dials are updated simultaneously in reverse sequence.
