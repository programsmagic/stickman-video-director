To navigate the fog without eyes, the algorithm relies on a compass called Gradient Descent. At every single coordinate, the AI feels the slope beneath its feet. The gradient is a mathematical vector pointing straight uphill toward greater error. So, the algorithm simply calculates the negative gradient—and takes one step in the exact opposite direction. The size of that step is controlled by a dial called the Learning Rate. Set the dial too low, and it will take a million years to descend an inch. Set it too high, and the algorithm leaps wildly across the chasm, overshooting the valley completely and tumbling into catastrophic divergence. Finding the sweet spot is the art of intelligence.
