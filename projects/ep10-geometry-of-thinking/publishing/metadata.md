1. **Visualizing the Loss Landscape of Neural Nets** (Li et al., NeurIPS 2018) — Filter normalization proving high-dimensional landscapes smooth into connected global basins.
2. **Identifying and Attacking the Saddle Point Problem in High-Dimensional Non-Convex Optimization** (Dauphin et al., NeurIPS 2014) — Mathematical proof that saddle points, not local minima, dominate high dimensions.
3. **Learning Representations by Back-Propagating Errors** (Rumelhart, Hinton & Williams, Nature 1986) — The foundational backpropagation algorithm and chain-rule formulation.
4. **Some Methods of Speeding Up the Convergence of Iteration Methods** (Polyak, USSR Computational Mathematics 1964) — Classical heavy-ball momentum.
5. **Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization** (Kingma & Ba, ICLR 2015) — First and second moment adaptive learning rate estimation.
