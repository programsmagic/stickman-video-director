As artificial intelligence grew smarter, it ran straight into a wall of pure physics! In a traditional dense model, every single parameter inside the brain must activate for every single syllable it speaks. If a model has six hundred billion parameters, and you ask it what is the capital of France, you have to blast electricity through all six hundred billion connections just to spit out the word Paris! Datacenters began consuming the electricity of entire cities. It was unsustainable, wildly expensive, and threatened to freeze AI progress in its tracks.

So engineers turned to an architectural masterpiece: Mixture of Experts, or MoE. Think of a bustling kitchen with sixty-four master chefs. When a customer orders a bowl of pasta, you don't wake up the sushi chef, the pastry chef, the barbecue pitmaster, and the French saucier! That would be madness! You only tap the pasta chef on the shoulder! In an MoE model, a lightning-fast router evaluates each incoming token and activates only the top expert subnetworks best suited for the job. The rest of the brain sleeps peacefully, saving enormous amounts of energy.

This architectural trick sparked a global technological earthquake. In DeepSeek-V3, there are six hundred and seventy-one billion total parameters — giving it vast encyclopedia-level knowledge. But for any single word it generates, it only fires thirty-seven billion parameters! That is a ninety-five percent discount on computing power! While Silicon Valley spent hundreds of millions of dollars brute-forcing monolithic models, open-weights researchers in China proved that elegant mathematical routing could match the world's best benchmarks at a fraction of the cost. The future of AI isn't just bigger computers. It's smarter algorithms!
